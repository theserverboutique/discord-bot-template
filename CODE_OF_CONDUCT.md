# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Server Boutique is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and inclusive environment for everyone.

We encourage collaboration, constructive feedback and respectful communication throughout this project.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming newcomers.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Accepting feedback gracefully.
- Helping others learn and grow.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam or unrelated promotional content.
- Any behaviour that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They may remove, edit or reject contributions, comments or other interactions that violate these guidelines.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from participating in the project.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled confidentially where possible.

---
